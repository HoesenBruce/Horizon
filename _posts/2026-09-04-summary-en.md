### [IFM Releases K2 Horizon Fleet of Six Open Language Models](https://ifm.ai/blog/k2/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

IFM has introduced K2 Horizon, a connected fleet of six open language models spanning diverse sizes and architectures. The release provides a fully open-stack implementation by including open training code and data details alongside the model weights. The project aims to offer transparent, self-hostable model options for the open-source AI ecosystem.

hackernews · karimf · Sep 3, 15:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49551760)

**「Background」** While many artificial intelligence releases publish open weights, they often omit the source code, training data recipes, and intermediate checkpoints required for full technical reproducibility. Fully open-stack projects address this gap by releasing the entire development pipeline under open licenses, allowing developers to inspect, reproduce, and self-host models across diverse compute environments \[tool-1-2, tool-1-3\].

**「Community Discussion」** Community members praised the release of a transparent, fully open AI stack, but questioned its benchmark performance and real-world utility. Critics pointed out that the dense 32B model lags behind rivals such as Qwen, while initial hands-on testing of the 3.7B variant revealed frequent coding errors, hallucinated APIs, and repetition loops.
